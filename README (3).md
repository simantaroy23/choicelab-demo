# ChoiceLab virtual store (interactive prototype)

**Try it:** https://simantaroy23.github.io/choicelab-demo/

A shopping simulation that measures how young adults who vape respond to U.S. e-cigarette policies. Instead of asking what people *would* do, it records what they actually buy, with money they get to keep.

## What a session looks like (about 5 minutes)

1. Eight short questions about vaping habits. The participant's typical weekly vaping spend becomes their budget for every shopping week.
2. Five shopping "weeks":
   - Week 1: the current market (baseline).
   - Weeks 2–5: one policy each, in a balanced (Williams) order:
     - Flavor ban (tobacco flavor only)
     - Disposable ban
     - Vape tax (+50% on licensed e-cigarettes)
     - Crackdown on illegal vapes (unlicensed seller is farther away and pricier)
3. Each week the participant can buy from a licensed store, walk to an unlicensed smoke shop, switch to cigarettes or nicotine gum, add some of their own money, or keep their budget. One week is paid out as a bonus.
4. The final screen shows the data recorded: units, spending, nicotine bought (mg), switching to illegal products, cigarettes or gum, and attention measures.

## Notes

- All stores, brands and products are fictional. Store and product images are AI-generated.
- The demo runs entirely in the browser. Nothing entered is collected or sent anywhere.
- English and Spanish.
