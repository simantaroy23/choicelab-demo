# ChoiceLab virtual store (interactive prototype)

**Try it:** https://simantaroy23.github.io/choicelab-demo/

A shopping simulation that measures how young adults who vape respond to U.S. e-cigarette policies. Instead of asking what people *would* do, it records what they actually buy, with money they get to keep.

## What a session looks like (about 5 minutes)

1. Eight short questions about vaping and other tobacco use. The participant's typical weekly spend on **all** tobacco and nicotine products becomes their budget for every shopping week.
2. Five shopping "weeks" in one licensed store (a walk-in store, or an online store for people who usually buy online):
   - Week 1: the current market (baseline).
   - Weeks 2–5: one policy each, in a balanced (Williams) order:
     - E-cigarette flavor ban (tobacco flavor only; menthol and mint removed)
     - Nicotine limit for e-cigarettes, 2% (20 mg/mL)
     - Pictorial health warnings on e-cigarettes and cigarettes
     - E-cigarette tax (+50% price)
3. Each week the participant can buy e-cigarettes, switch to cigarettes, cigarillos, nicotine pouches or nicotine gum, add some of their own money, or keep their budget. They can also say they would get products from another source (friend, another store, online, social media or other) and write what and where. One week is paid out as a bonus.
4. The final screen shows the data recorded: units by product type, spending, nicotine bought (mg), stated other-source use, and attention measures.

## Notes

- All stores, brands and products are fictional. Store and product images are AI-generated; warning labels use FDA's required nicotine statement (e-cigarettes) and one of FDA's 2020 cigarette warning statements, with simple illustrations.
- The demo runs entirely in the browser. Nothing entered is collected or sent anywhere.
- English and Spanish.
